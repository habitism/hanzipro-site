This Privacy Policy applies to **HanziPro: Learn Chinese**, developed by Wenfeng Deng.

## Information and storage

HanziPro does not require an account operated by the developer, and the developer does not collect personal information or learning data from the app.

Your learning progress, review records and app settings are stored on your device. Depending on the features and settings you choose, this information may also be included in a device backup or stored in your personal cloud account. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

## Sharing and security

The developer does not sell, rent or share your personal information. Because the developer does not collect or transmit your learning data to a developer-operated server, it is protected by your device and personal cloud account security settings. You are responsible for keeping your device and cloud account secure.

## Retention and deletion

Information stored by HanziPro remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Information stored in a personal cloud account can be managed or deleted through the relevant account settings. As HanziPro does not provide a developer-operated account, there is no separate HanziPro account to delete.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when HanziPro's features or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
